MAC 2026 Baseball Program
----------------------------------
Youth baseball will be under the auspices of the Moanalua Athletic Club (MAC) & [POLICE ACTIVITY LEAGUE (PAL)](https://www.honolulupd.org/pal/){:target="_blank"}.

The MAC baseball program is primarily for boys and girls residing in the Moanalua area (Fort Shafter to Red Hill and mauka of the freeway) and/or attend Moanalua Elementary School.)

Baseball Teams are for boys & girls, and the age determination date is the child’s age on January 1, 2026.

MAC Baseball divisions and age groups are as follows (age on January 1, 2026):

| Division | Age | Practice Days |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pinto & Jr. Pinto  | 5, 6, 7 | Mondays & Fridays|
| Mustang | 9, 10 |Monday or Tuesdays, & Thursdays| 

Please note that we will not form a team unless we have a minimum number of coaches and players. The minimum number of players is 9 and the maximum is 16.  

The priority for selection is as follows:
1. Head coach and assistant coaches children
1. Players who attend Moanalua Elementary School.
1. Players living in the Moanalua Elementary District
1. Players who have obtained a Geographic Exception to Moanalua Elementary School
1. All others in the age groups

MAC Fees
----
The registration fee for all age groups will be $60.
These fees cover the cost of:

* Providing player team jersey, and cap
* Any surplus will be go purchasing baseball equipment and supplies

<span style="color:red">Payment is due on the first day of practice (June 1).</span>

Uniforms
--------
Team jersey and cap will be provided to players this season.

* Baseball pants and socks will not be provided this year. Players are responsible for providing their own baseball pants BLACK colored socks for games.

Payment
------------
A completed registration form and payment must be received before a player is assigned to a team.
<span style="color:red">Payment is due on the first day of practice (June 1).</span>

Check Policy
------------
Checks returned unpaid by your bank will be returned to you by the program coordinator.
Your check will not be re-deposited.
Within one week of notification, you must make arrangements with the coordinator (or any board member) to pay the registration fee plus $20.00 (bank charges) by either: 1) cash or 2) another check.
Should your second check be returned, all future payments (regardless of activity) must be made in cash.
If you have any questions regarding this policy, please check with the program coordinator.
