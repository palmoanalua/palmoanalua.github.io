GOT BASEBALL???…A LITTLE LATE, BUT WE DO!

We have a Head Coach and assistant coaches for the Mustang team!
We may also have a coach for the Jr. Pintos and Pinto teams!

Youth baseball will be under the auspices of the Moanalua Athletic Club (MAC) & POLICE ACTIVITY LEAGUE (PAL).
The MAC baseball program is primarily for boys and girls residing in the Moanalua area (Fort Shafter to Red Hill and mauka of the freeway) and/or attend Moanalua Elementary School.)

The season is expected to start the second Saturday in June and end the last Saturday in July.
We will have a Mustang team, ages 9 & 10 and a combined Pinto/Jr. Pinto team,ages 5-7.

Baseball Teams are for boys & girls, and the age determination date is the child’s age on January 1, 2026.

See our [baseball](/baseball) page for more information and sign-up link.
